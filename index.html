📐 FarmIntel: System Architecture & Data Pipeline

From Agronomic Prediction to Profit-Driven Crop Planning & Continuous Calibration

PILLAR 1
🌐 Enhanced Dynamic Inputs
  • Geospatial & Climate API: Real-time Open-Meteo live weather + district GPS mapping
  • Market Intelligence API: Live APMC mandi commodity rates & 30-day price momentum
  • Input Cost Tracking: Granular unit costs for certified rootstocks, fertilizers, and trellis labor
  • Farmer Field Profile: Land size, soil type, water source, and capital budget
➔
PILLAR 2
⚙️ Two-Stage ML Pipeline
Stage 1: Agronomic Viability ("What Can Grow")

Evaluates plot conditions against physiological crop affinities (soil pH/drainage, rainfall, water status, pest-break rotation). Predicts baseline yield range.

Stage 2: Profit Optimization ("What Will Sell & Profit")

Deducts itemized input costs from expected APMC mandi revenue to compute Net Profit & Return on Investment (ROI %). Ranks options to maximize farmer returns within budget.

➔
PILLAR 3
🔄 Continuous Learning Loop
  • Post-Harvest Field Input: Farmers record actual harvested volume (tonnes/acre) and actual APMC price received
  • Bayesian Model Calibration: System computes localized yield and price multipliers
  • Adaptive Accuracy: Future predictions automatically calibrate to real-world plot conditions over time
✨ Engineered for farmer empowerment and academic project review rigor.
